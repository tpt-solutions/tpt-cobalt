## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-16

### Added

- Initial release of `tpt-eng-props-fuels`: fuel properties for energy and combustion calculations —
  lower/higher heating values, density, stoichiometric air–fuel ratio, and CO₂ emission factors for
  natural gas, hydrogen, hydrogen–natural-gas blends, and diesel, with `no_std` support.
