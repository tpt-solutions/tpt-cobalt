### Added

- Initial release of `tpt-eng-plot`: engineering-style plots — multi-series XY line/scatter charts (`XyPlot`), bar charts of named results (`ResultChart`), and 2D cross-section drawings (`SectionDrawing`) — exportable to PNG or SVG, built on `plotters`.
