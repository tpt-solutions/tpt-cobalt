## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-16

### Added

- Initial release of `tpt-eng-geometry`: core 3D geometry primitives and operations for engineering applications, built on `glam`, covering points/vectors, frames and affine transforms, curves (line/circle/arc/Bézier), surfaces (plane/sphere/cylinder), intersections, projections, and geometric queries (distances, angles, triangle area, bounding boxes).
