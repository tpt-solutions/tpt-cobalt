DESIGN DOCUMENT: tpt-formal (FORMAL VERIFICATION FOUNDATION)

BEFORE YOU WRITE ANYTHING BELOW: check tpt-rust-map/registry.toml (in the
sibling tpt-rust-map repo) for an existing foundation or external crate
before adding a new one here. Every crate name in this repo carries the
tpt-for- prefix. A crate is only a valid wrap target if its CURRENT
license is MIT OR Apache-2.0 (or more permissive) — Apache-2.0-ONLY is
always disqualifying, no exceptions (see tpt-rust-map/docs/adr/0007). If
it passes that check, default to wrapping; reimplementation still needs
one written reason. Prefer many small crates + a thin umbrella crate over
one broad crate. Every crate: license = "MIT OR Apache-2.0".

PROJECT OVERVIEW
tpt-formal consolidates tpt-solutions' formal-verification and design-by-
contract primitives — currently spread across tpt-zero-formal (~16
contract/type-safety crates) and tpt-formal-lab (~8 verification crates) —
into one dedicated repo. This pillar was added after the audit turned up a
substantial, distinct body of work in this space that deserved the same
treatment as Math/Science/Engineering, not a fold-in. Every safety-critical
vertical in tpt-solutions (tpt-flight-control, tpt-dynamo, tpt-vanguard,
tpt-chassis, tpt-servo, tpt-relay, and others) is a plausible future
consumer of these primitives.

ECOSYSTEM GAP JUSTIFICATION
Design-by-contract, refinement types, type-level safety encoding, and
deterministic verification tooling are not well served by mature, broadly-
adopted permissive crates in the Rust ecosystem the way linear algebra or
statistics are — this is largely genuine build territory, not wrap
territory. Where an external crate does exist for a piece of this (e.g. an
SMT solver binding for tpt-for-smt-lite's bridge), prefer wrapping it — see
the per-crate wrap-candidate notes in CRATE INVENTORY below for the specific
findings (SMT bridge, compile-time assertions, and two typestate facets all
have live wrap candidates; design-by-contract and deterministic-sim were
checked and confirmed as build territory).
tpt-formal-lab's tpt-exact-math does NOT live here — it moved to tpt-math
as tpt-math-exact, since exact/interval arithmetic is a numeric primitive
useful far beyond formal verification.

CORE PRINCIPLES
The grouping below consolidates ~24 legacy crates into 13, merging facets
that are genuinely inseparable (a contract system needs pre/post/invariant
together to be coherent; type-level safety encoding techniques like
phantom/ghost/newtype/bounded are facets of one toolkit) while keeping
everything else at its existing one-crate-one-technique granularity. This
grouping is a judgment call, not derived from audit evidence the way
Math's tpt-math-prob-* split was — see docs/adr/0006 in tpt-rust-map. Split
further, per-crate, the moment a grouped facet wants independent
versioning sooner than expected; don't force a merge a real consumer is
fighting. Dual-licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. no_std where the underlying
legacy crate was already no_std (marked per crate below) — most of this
pillar's contract/type-safety primitives are no_std by nature since they're
meant to be usable in the embedded/safety-critical verticals that motivate
this pillar. Six further crates (tpt-for-model-check, tpt-for-sat,
tpt-for-vcgen, tpt-for-abstract-interp, tpt-for-symbolic-exec,
tpt-for-runtime-verify) were added after a later ecosystem-gap pass found
verification techniques the 13-crate consolidation didn't cover at all —
these have no legacy predecessor, so they sit outside the "~24 into 13"
consolidation count: 13 consolidated + 6 new = 19 total.

CRATE INVENTORY

  tpt-for-contract
    Build: consolidates precond/postcond/invariant/loop-inv — tightly
    coupled facets of one design-by-contract concern. no_std. Consolidates
    tpt-zero-formal's tpt-zero-precond, tpt-zero-postcond,
    tpt-zero-invariant, tpt-zero-loop-inv. Depends on: tpt-math-numeric.
    Wrap check: `contracts`, the ecosystem's most popular design-by-contract
    crate, is MPL-2.0 — disqualified. No compatible alternative found;
    confirms build-not-wrap here.

  tpt-for-refinement
    Build: refinement types. no_std. Consolidates tpt-zero-formal's
    tpt-zero-refinement. Depends on: tpt-for-contract.

  tpt-for-typestate
    Build: consolidates type-level/phantom/ghost/newtype/bounded/safe-cast
    — one coherent "type-level safety encoding" toolkit. no_std.
    Consolidates tpt-zero-formal's tpt-zero-type-level, tpt-zero-phantom,
    tpt-zero-ghost, tpt-zero-newtype, tpt-zero-bounded, tpt-zero-safe-cast.
    Wrap candidates: `bounded-integer` (ISC, no_std) for the bounded facet;
    `nutype` (MIT, gained no_std support in recent releases — verify against
    this workspace's rust-version 1.75 before adopting) for newtype/safe-cast.
    `typestate` (MIT OR Apache-2.0) exists but is pre-1.0 and covers only
    state-machine typestate, not the full toolkit — doesn't override build.

  tpt-for-witness
    Build: witness types. no_std. Consolidates tpt-zero-formal's
    tpt-zero-witness. Depends on: tpt-for-typestate.

  tpt-for-assert-const
    Build: compile-time assertions. no_std. Consolidates tpt-zero-formal's
    tpt-zero-assert-const.
    Wrap candidate: `static_assertions` (MIT OR Apache-2.0, no_std, stable
    at 1.1.0 for years) does exactly this — prefer wrapping; reimplementation
    needs a written reason.

  tpt-for-smt-lite
    Build: lightweight SMT bridge. Consolidates tpt-zero-formal's
    tpt-zero-smt-lite and tpt-formal-lab's out-smt-bridge. Wrap candidates:
    `easy-smt` (MIT OR Apache-2.0) — subprocess SMT-LIB2 term/assertion
    builder driving any SMT-LIB2 solver binary, matches this crate's scope
    closely; primary pick. `rsmt2` (MIT/Apache-2.0) — heavier alternative
    with solver-specific extensions, fallback if easy-smt proves too thin.
    `z3` (MIT, official Microsoft bindings) — only if an in-process solver
    is needed instead of a subprocess; heavier build (links Z3's C++ lib).
    Rejected: no serious Yices2 binding exists, and Yices2 itself is
    GPLv3 — any wrap would be tainted regardless of the binding's license.

  tpt-for-verified-ode
    Build: verified ODE solving, pairs with tpt-science's tpt-sci-ode.
    Consolidates tpt-formal-lab's tpt-verified-ode. Depends on:
    tpt-sci-ode (from the tpt-science repo), tpt-for-contract.

  tpt-for-verified-algorithms
    Build: verified algorithm implementations. Consolidates
    tpt-formal-lab's tpt-verified-algorithms. Depends on: tpt-for-contract.

  tpt-for-proof-ast
    Build: proof AST representation. Consolidates tpt-formal-lab's
    tpt-proof-ast. Watch: `egg` (MIT, e-graph/equality-saturation) may be
    relevant if this needs rewriting/equality reasoning over the AST rather
    than pure representation — open question, not decided here.

  tpt-for-det-proptest
    Build: deterministic property-based testing. Consolidates
    tpt-formal-lab's tpt-det-proptest. Wrap candidate: `bolero` (MIT) —
    fuzz/property front-end with deterministic corpus replay across
    libfuzzer/AFL/honggfuzz; could sit underneath a thin wrapper that pins
    seeds/config for CI determinism instead of hand-rolled generators.

  tpt-for-deterministic-sim
    Build: deterministic simulation harness. Consolidates tpt-formal-lab's
    tpt-deterministic-sim. Wrap candidates: `turmoil` (tokio-rs, MIT) for
    deterministic multi-host network/fault simulation; `loom` (tokio-rs,
    MIT) for concurrency permutation testing — complementary, not
    overlapping. Rejected: `madsim` is the closest single-crate fit but is
    Apache-2.0-only — disqualified per ADR 0007, no exceptions.

  tpt-for-redundancy
    Build: redundancy/fault-tolerance primitives. no_std. Consolidates
    tpt-formal-lab's tpt-redundancy.

  tpt-for-trace-macros
    Build: trace/instrumentation macros for verification. no_std.
    Consolidates tpt-formal-lab's tpt-trace-macros.

  tpt-for-model-check
    Build: explicit-state model checking (safety/liveness/linearizability)
    for concurrent/distributed protocol logic — exhaustive state-space
    search, distinct from tpt-for-deterministic-sim's sampled simulation and
    tpt-for-det-proptest's random property testing. No legacy predecessor in
    tpt-zero-formal/tpt-formal-lab — new capability, not a consolidation.
    Wrap: `stateright` (MIT, 14.4M downloads, actively maintained, last
    published 2025-07) — actor library with an embedded exhaustive model
    checker and linearizability tester; a genuinely excellent, mature
    alternative already exists, so wrap it rather than rebuild it.
    NOT YET checked against tpt-rust-map/registry.toml (sibling repo
    unavailable in this environment) — do that before registering.

  tpt-for-sat
    Build: from-scratch, pure-Rust CDCL SAT solver — a lighter-weight layer
    under/alongside the SMT bridge for pure-boolean verification conditions,
    and useful standalone for bounded model checking's boolean encoding. No
    legacy predecessor — new capability. Checked and rejected as dependencies
    (not as design references): `cadical`/`rustsat-cadical` (MIT, excellent,
    but FFI to C++ — wrong shape for a pillar feeding embedded/safety-
    critical verticals, regardless of license); `varisat` (MIT/Apache-2.0,
    but unpublished since 2020-09 — too stale to trust for a correctness-
    critical solver engine); `splr` (MPL-2.0, disqualified outright);
    `batsat` (MIT, pure Rust, no FFI — closest fit shape-wise, but a
    single-maintainer, MiniSat-vintage port, not competitive with a modern
    CDCL solver). Written reason to build: no modern, actively-maintained,
    competitive, pure-Rust (no native-library link) CDCL solver exists in
    the ecosystem. NOT YET checked against tpt-rust-map/registry.toml.

  tpt-for-vcgen
    Build: verification-condition generation — computes weakest-
    precondition-style proof obligations from tpt-for-contract's pre/post/
    invariant annotations, to hand to tpt-for-smt-lite as dischargeable
    goals. No legacy predecessor — this is the missing link that turns
    "we have contracts" and "we have an SMT bridge" into an actual
    "prove this function correct" pipeline; neither existing crate connects
    to the other today. Checked directly: no Rust crate for this exists at
    all (Why3 and Boogie are the classic tools for this technique, but both
    are separate languages/toolchains, not Cargo dependencies — Boogie is
    MIT but its own IL, Why3 is LGPL). Clean whitespace, not a case of
    "something exists but is weak." NOT YET checked against
    tpt-rust-map/registry.toml.

  tpt-for-abstract-interp
    Build: generic abstract-interpretation framework (interval/sign/other
    numeric domains, fixpoint iteration) for static analysis of numeric/
    control-flow properties, complementing tpt-for-vcgen/tpt-for-smt-lite
    for properties that don't need full first-order reasoning. No legacy
    predecessor. Only real prior art: `sparta` (MIT, a Rust port of Meta's
    C++ abstract-interpretation library behind ReDex) — license is clean,
    but it self-describes as experimental with an unstable API, has
    negligible adoption (3,956 downloads total), and hasn't published since
    2023-05. Written reason to build: not a foundation to build a
    safety-critical verification pillar on. NOT YET checked against
    tpt-rust-map/registry.toml.

  tpt-for-symbolic-exec
    Build: whole-program symbolic execution over compiled code for bug-
    finding/property-proving — a different technique from model checking
    (protocol/actor state space) and SAT (pure boolean formulas). No legacy
    predecessor. Only real candidate: `haybale` (MIT end to end, including
    its boolector/Boolector dependency) — license is clean, but single-lab
    maintainer (PLSysSec) and thin adoption (27.7K total / 124 recent
    downloads). Written reason to build: not broadly-adopted/actively-
    maintained enough to build safety-critical tooling on top of. Worth
    reading as a design reference (LLVM-IR-level symbolic execution over an
    SMT backend is sound, well-trodden) without taking it as a dependency.
    NOT YET checked against tpt-rust-map/registry.toml.

  tpt-for-runtime-verify
    Build: checks a temporal/stream specification against live event data —
    distinct from tpt-for-trace-macros, which emits instrumentation but
    checks nothing. No legacy predecessor. Only real candidate:
    `rtlola-interpreter` (CISPA/Saarland) — Apache-2.0-ONLY, disqualified per
    ADR 0007 no exceptions, and niche (24K downloads) independent of the
    license problem. Written reason to build: no MIT/Apache-2.0-or-more-
    permissive option exists. Clean-room note: if RTLola's public spec is
    used as a design reference, implement independently from the published
    algorithm, not by porting its Apache-2.0-only source — keeps the
    resulting dual-license claim honest. NOT YET checked against
    tpt-rust-map/registry.toml.

GROUPING CAVEAT
This 19-crate split (13 original + 6 added after further ecosystem-gap
research — see the six entries above for wrap/build rationale) is
provisional (see tpt-rust-map/docs/adr/0006). If,
during implementation, tpt-for-refinement or tpt-for-witness turn out to
need independent release cadence sooner than expected, split them out of
tpt-for-contract/tpt-for-typestate respectively rather than forcing the
merge.

REPO CONVENTIONS
Cargo workspace under crates/, one crate per directory, seeded from
tpt-rust-map/template/. Every crate registered in tpt-rust-map/registry.toml
before implementation starts.
